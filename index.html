Our story

The founders of the label

About the founders

Ugobest Music Worldwide was founded on 21 May 2005 by Mr. Olatunji Adebayo on the left and Mr. Tilako Dateh on the right. The label has shaped some of the most successful Afro artists in the world and continues to identify fresh talent.

Mr. Tilako is now the CEO, while Mr. Olatunji stepped away in 2013.

Maxwell

Known as Swag Boi, Maxwell grew to fame in 2013 with the hit single Omayile and continues to push the sound of the label forward.

Obafemi

Also known as Dollar Boi, he joined the label in February 2023 and was nominated for a Grammy for his album Say Less.

Mark
